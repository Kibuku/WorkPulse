How WorkPulse understands your work

WorkPulse combines small local signals into a proposed work story. A signal is evidence, not certainty.
Active app and windowSamples the foreground window every five seconds and groups continuous activity into sessions.
Idle timeStops treating time as active after 60 seconds without keyboard or mouse input.
File changesNotices files created, changed, moved, or deleted in selected work folders. It records metadata, not every document's contents.
Browser and calendar contextReads supported active browser tabs when permission allows and uses an optional calendar feed to understand meetings.
Semantic observationsLocally proposes workflow stages and possible friction from repeated metadata patterns. You can confirm or dismiss every hypothesis.
Your personal layer stays yours.

Raw titles, URLs, file paths, personal activity, captures, corrections, and detailed history remain on this machine.

What WorkPulse does not collect
  • No keystroke logging.
  • No continuous screenshots or screen video.
  • No silent upload of raw personal evidence.
  • No organisation access simply because WorkPulse is installed.
